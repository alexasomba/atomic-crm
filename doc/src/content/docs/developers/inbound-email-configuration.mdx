---
title: Configuring inbound email
---

Inbound email uses Cloudflare Email Routing and the Worker `email()` handler.
The configured staging address is `crm@atomic-crm.asomba.com`.

Cloudflare stores the raw MIME message in R2, records an idempotent event in D1,
and queues processing. `postal-mime` extracts the sender, recipients, body, and
attachments. The sender must match a CRM sales user and one of the To/Cc/Bcc
recipients must match a CRM contact. The contact receives a note and activity
owned by the matching sales user; unmatched events remain visible to
administrators for replay.

For staging, send from the email address of a CRM sales user and CC
`crm@atomic-crm.asomba.com` while retaining the CRM contact in To or Cc. A
message sent only to the CRM address has no contact recipient and is correctly
reported as unmatched.

Set `VITE_INBOUND_EMAIL_ADDRESS` for the frontend display. Configure the
`EMAIL` send binding and verify SPF, DKIM, and DMARC before enabling outbound
Better Auth verification and reset messages.
